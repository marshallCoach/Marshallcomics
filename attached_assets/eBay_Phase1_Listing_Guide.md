## STAGE 2 — TOMORROW MORNING, natural daylight

### Photo setup (5 minutes, before the first book)
- **Location:** near a window, indirect natural light — not direct sun (creates glare/hotspots on the glossy cover), not artificial light (yellow color cast makes grading look worse than it is)
- **Background:** plain, neutral, non-reflective — a piece of black or white poster board works better than a table surface with visible texture/wood grain
- **Camera angle:** directly overhead or straight-on, not at an angle — angled shots distort the cover and look unprofessional
- **Phone settings:** turn OFF flash (causes glare on the plastic bag), turn ON grid lines (helps keep the shot square)

### Per-book shot list (3 photos minimum, do this for every book)
1. **Front cover, full book in frame, straight-on** — this is the listing's main photo, get it right
2. **Back cover, same angle** — buyers want to see the back, especially for grading trust
3. **Spine close-up** — shows any spine stress/rolling, critical for VF/FN grade claims to be believable
4. **If any damage exists** (water stain, tear, missing piece) — a fourth close-up photo of the specific flaw. This protects you from a return dispute later — showing the flaw upfront is better than a buyer finding it and leaving negative feedback.

### Workflow to avoid mixing up photos
- Photograph one book completely (all angles) before moving to the next
- After each book, either rename the photos immediately on your phone or move them to a folder named for that book's title+issue — don't let 60 photos of "IMG_4821" through "IMG_4881" sit unsorted, you will not remember which is which by book #12

## STAGE 3 — AFTER PHOTOS, listing

### Individual listing vs. bulk — the real answer for 19 unique comics
**Do NOT use eBay's bulk CSV upload (File Exchange) for this batch.** That tool is built for high-volume identical SKUs (same item, many units) — your 19 books are 19 unique items with unique photos and unique conditions. Forcing them through a bulk template adds setup complexity for zero time savings at this volume. **List these one at a time through the normal "Sell" flow** — at 19 items, individual listing is faster than building and debugging a CSV template.

*(If your collection grows to hundreds of listings later, bulk tools become worth the setup cost — not at 19.)*

### The listing template — from your own strategy doc, use exactly this format
```
TITLE: [Series Title] #[Issue] ([Year]) — [Writer] — Raw [Condition]

DESCRIPTION:
[Series Title] #[Issue]
Writer: [Name] · Artist: [Name]
Published: [Year] by [Publisher]

Condition: [Your honest grade from Stage 1] — Ships bagged and boarded
Pages: Full color · Ships within 2 business days

[ONE sentence: why this issue matters — first issue, key arc, notable run]

Buyer protection: 30-day returns accepted.
```
